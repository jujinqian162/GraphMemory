# Selected HotpotQA R-GCN ablations

`configs/experiment/hotpotqa_rgcn_ablation_selected.yaml` is the retained selected-variant preset. Invoke it with `--config-name=hotpotqa_rgcn_ablation_selected`, a unique name, and the desired profile. Variant invalidation and `full_rgcn` baseline aliases are derived by the typed planner and persisted in run state; there are no copied alias artifacts.
